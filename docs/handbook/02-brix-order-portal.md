# Brix Order Portal — Customer Ordering, Billing & Resources

> Part I · User Guide · Owner: Sky Pace · Last reviewed: 2026-07-22

This chapter is the **staff version** of the customer manual for the Brix Order Portal at https://orders.brixbev.com — the customer-facing B2B portal where customers (and staff acting on their behalf) place orders, track deliveries, view and pay invoices, manage tank rentals, file service requests, and read the self-help Resources library. It covers everything a customer sees, plus staff-only notes on what actually happens behind each screen (Service Fusion jobs, the QBO mirror, lifecycle emails). For the staff console itself see [Brix Order /admin — Staff Console](#/03-brix-order-admin).

## Signing in, passwords & recovery

New users get a **welcome email** with a "Set your password" link. That link lands on the portal's own `/set-password` page (it deliberately does NOT round-trip through Supabase's redirect flow — the shared project's Site URL is the gateway, which used to strand customers on the wrong login).

Password rules (enforced by the shared Supabase project): **at least 8 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number, and a symbol.** The set-password page pre-validates and shows friendly copy; all password fields have a show/hide eye toggle.

Recovery paths:

1. **Forgot password** — link on `/login`; enter the email, a branded reset email delivers a one-time `/set-password` link. It always claims success (anti-enumeration), so an unknown email simply gets no mail.
2. **Expired/used welcome link** — resend the welcome email from `/admin` (customer detail → Users tab), optionally with a password reset.

> **Staff note:** welcome/reset links are one-time tokens. If a customer says the link "doesn't work," resend rather than debug — emails sent before 2026-07-09 carry old-style links that landed on the gateway hub. Temp passwords generated by the admin tools already satisfy the four-character-class policy.

## Home dashboard & navigation

After sign-in, customers land on **Home** — recent activity, open balance at a glance, and shortcuts into ordering, invoices, and service. The left sidebar is the main navigation: Shop/Catalog, Orders, Invoices, Statements, Pay, Autopay, Cylinders, Equipment, Service, Resources, and Account.

> **Staff note:** staff (superadmins) do NOT land on Home — they land on **/admin/quick-order**, the phone-order launchpad. Acting as a customer is a deliberate step, never the default.

## Account switcher & memberships

Access is membership-based: one user can belong to many customers (`orders.customer_memberships`), each with a per-customer role. The active customer is **persisted server-side**, so a selection follows the user across tabs and devices.

- **Customers** with more than one membership get a dropdown at the top of the sidebar; single-account users see no switcher at all. Locations are the sub-accounts under a master account — there is no separate "Locations" screen.
- **Staff** never get a multi-customer dropdown. The sidebar shows a static **"Ordering for \<customer\>"** chip (with a deactivated tag when applicable) and a "Change customer →" link to /admin/quick-order. Staff can star favorite customers from the admin customer detail page; favorites sort first on the Quick order list.

> **Staff note:** "Act as customer" (admin customer detail header, or Start order on /admin/quick-order) switches the RLS context — the whole app then shows that customer's data exactly as a real member would see it. Everything you do while acting-as (cart, orders, payments) is done AS that customer.

## Shopping: catalog, My Collection, backorders

- The **catalog** (`orders.catalog`, mirrored from `ops.qbo_items`; names match Service Fusion) supports browse/search; **Specs** on a product opens package details, sizes, and finished-volume math.
- **My Collection** is the customer's shortlist of regulars — the fastest reorder path.
- **Inventory never blocks ordering.** The Add button is always enabled regardless of QBO `qty_on_hand`. Zero-stock non-gas items show an informational "Backordered — we'll confirm timing" note. Gas items (category `Gas` or SKU `CO80*/BR80*/NI80*`) show no stock badges at all.
- Pickup items (`PU*` SKUs) are hidden from the shop grid and My Collection — they only enter an order through the tank-return flow below.

> **Staff note:** the always-enabled Add button is deliberate (sessions 1.45/1.48) — most gas rows carry QBO qty 0 and the old "Out of stock" disable literally prevented cylinder orders. `submit-order` never checked stock server-side anyway.

## Cart, tank returns & submitting an order

1. Add items and quantities, open the **Cart**.
2. **Preview** (dry-run) shows prices and an estimated total including fee lines, without submitting.
3. **Review & submit.** If the cart contains gas, the portal asks **"Empty tanks to return?"** — No goes straight to the submit modal; Yes opens per-type steppers over the seven `PU*` pickup items, then a confirmation ("You have selected N tanks for return & pickup"). Confirmed pickups ride the order as $0 lines.
4. Submit. On-screen confirmation plus a confirmation email with a permanent order-status link (`/orders/:order_id`).

> **Staff note — fees are ESTIMATES.** The portal computes fee lines (fuel, hazmat, CRV, SSB, etc.) for the on-screen estimate, order record, and confirmation email only. They are **never sent to Service Fusion** — SF's own job-level fee engine bills them natively (sending them both broke every order for two weeks in June 2026 AND would double-charge). The invoice is the final word on what's billed.

> **Staff note — SF job categories.** Portal orders (including admin quick-orders) create SF jobs tagged **"Brix Web Order"**; Chloe's phone orders are tagged **"Brix Phone Order"** (env `SF_ORDER_JOB_CATEGORY` / `SF_PHONE_ORDER_JOB_CATEGORY`). Both categories must exist in SF Settings → Job Categories; an SF rejection retries once without the category so a missing setting never blocks an order.

## Order tracking & lifecycle emails

**Orders** lists order history with live status: **Submitted → Scheduled → Out for delivery / Delivered**, plus **Cancelled**. The status page URL from the confirmation email is stable and always live.

Behind it, the `order-lifecycle-check` poller runs **every 5 minutes** (SF has no webhooks) and drives four emails:

| Email | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Confirmation | Sent at submit (poller backfills if the submit-time send failed) |
| Scheduled | The SF job HAS a scheduled date (deliberately not status-name matching — custom SF statuses kept breaking it) |
| Out for delivery | SF status observed by the poller |
| Delivered | SF status matches Done/Delivered/Invoiced patterns |

Cancelled SF jobs (or jobs deleted in SF) flip the portal order to Cancelled within one poller tick and stop further emails.

> **Staff note — recipients.** All lifecycle emails go to the **submitter + the customer's Primary email (`billing_email`), deduped**, BCC service@brixbev.com. So an order you enter on a customer's behalf still notifies the customer's contact. `email_events` claims each send exactly-once.

## Invoices & statements

**Invoices** reads the QBO mirror (`ops.qbo_invoices` via `orders.v_invoices_all`). Filters: **All / Open / Paid / Past due**; the four summary cards (Open balance, Past due, Open count, Paid count) are clickable filters. Click into an invoice for the full branded document with line items, dates, terms, and balance due.

**Download PDF** and **Print** emit the **canonical server-rendered document** — byte-identical to what DocuPost mails and what invoice emails attach (`assembleInvoicePdf`, one shared renderer so paper and download can never drift). Post-delivery invoice emails attach the PDF as `brix-invoice-<doc#>.pdf`.

**Statements** shows account activity and outstanding balance with aging; Download/Print likewise emit the mailed statement PDF.

Special documents: monthly tank-rental invoices (BTRF-*) show cylinder counts; **CR-ADJ** invoices render the full Tank Rental Audit document (counts, photos, signatures) — see [CO₂ Cylinder Audit PWA — Driver Field Guide](#/04-driver-cylinder-audit).

## Paying online

From **Pay** (or "Pay this invoice" on any open invoice):

1. Select one or more open invoices — several can be settled in one payment.
2. Choose a **saved payment method** (always masked to last four, e.g. "Checking ···1234") or enter details, depending on the account's rail.
3. Review the exact total, confirm. A green-strip **receipt email** goes out immediately to the customer's Primary email + the submitter (deduped, BCC service@brixbev.com).

After paying, the invoice shows **Processing** until the QBO mirror flips it to **Paid**. Processing never masks Paid — the badge only renders while the mirror still shows the invoice open.

> **Staff note — the honest QBO-mirror lag.** The paid/open status comes from `ops.qbo_invoices`, which brix-order cannot write. The processor posts the payment into QBO pre-applied; `pay-invoices` then nudges the QBO CDC sync so the flip is usually near-immediate, with the 15-minute `qbo-cdc-sync` cron as the backstop. If the processor batches the charge, QBO doesn't have it yet and the flip waits for the batch to post. The receipt email is the immediate acknowledgment; the invoice status follows on sync. **Do not pay an invoice twice because it still reads open minutes after a payment.**

> **Staff note — adding a payment method.** "Add or update a payment method" opens the payment processor's secure hosted window *inside* the portal (wallet screen only — saving a card there does not charge anything). Raw card/bank numbers never touch our code (PCI); the portal only ever sees last-four. The actual charge always goes through OUR tracked Pay button so `orders.payments` records it — payments completed inside a processor-hosted page bypass our records entirely (this bit us once; see session 1.64). Rail details and per-customer processor cutovers live in [SOP-4 · Billing & Payments](#/24-sop-billing-payments).

**Returned payments:** a bounced bank payment reopens the invoice and a returned-payment fee applies per account terms. Detection and bookkeeping are staff workflows on /admin/payments — see [SOP-4](#/24-sop-billing-payments).

## Autopay

Recurring rental charges (monthly cylinder/tank rental, equipment rental) can be enrolled in **autopay**. Enrolled rental invoices show "On auto pay"; manage or cancel from the invoice panel or the **Autopay** page. Autopay applies to recurring rentals only — product orders and one-off invoices are always paid manually.

> **Staff note:** customers on the legacy-processor bridge see a bridge panel on /autopay reflecting their live autopay state (on/off, method, schedule) with a "Manage autopay & payment methods" button opening the processor's hosted window; the new-rails enrollment UI is hidden for them so double-enrollment is impossible.

## Cylinders & tank rentals

The **Cylinders** page shows how many cylinders (CO₂, beer-gas blend, nitrogen) the records say are on site, by type, with the monthly rental rate and a full history of deliveries, pickups, and audit adjustments (adjustment rows link to their CR-ADJ invoice).

> **Staff note:** counts are derived entirely from QBO invoice lines — latest BTRF balance + deliveries − pickups, with a synthetic zero anchor for buckets that have activity but no BTRF history yet. If a customer disputes a count, check the transaction history and recent CR-ADJ invoices first, then schedule a field audit ([driver guide](#/04-driver-cylinder-audit)).

## Equipment

The **Equipment** page lists the Brix equipment on site and any equipment rental charges. Equipment problems go through a service request, not the order flow.

## Service requests

**Service** submits a ticket for machine issues, gas problems, pickups — anything needing a technician or driver. Submitted requests and their statuses are listed on the same page. Urgent equipment-down situations should phone the office.

> **Staff note:** service requests **auto-push to Service Fusion on submit** — equipment issues become SF category "Service Call", refills "Product Delivery". The service@brixbev.com notification email shows "✓ Already in Service Fusion as Job #N"; a manual push button only appears if the auto-push failed. Mr. Bubbles and Chloe file through the same pipeline (see [AI Assistants](#/05-voice-ai-assistants)).

## Resources — the self-help library

**/resources** is the in-portal help library: troubleshooting guides (fountain, bar gun, CO₂, ice machines, frozen/juice/brewed), cleaning & maintenance schedules, BIB how-to videos, and ordering/billing guides. A hero button opens **Mr. Bubbles in a big window** for guided troubleshooting.

> **Staff note:** the library is served live from `orders.kb_documents` — edit any article on /admin → Knowledge and it is live immediately, no deploy. The "Show in the customer Resources library" checkbox controls visibility (hidden docs still answer in chat via RAG; `phone-teachings` is permanently hidden). The page requires sign-in; the public marketing library remains resources.alamedasoda.com.

## Account settings, email slots & team

**Account / Settings** covers profile (name, phone, password), billing email routing, and team management.

### The four email slots

Each customer has up to four addresses, and each address independently selects what it receives (Invoices / Statements / Reminders):

| UI label | Column | Routing role |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Email | `billing_email` | `billing` — gets everything by default; also the lifecycle-email + receipt recipient |
| Secondary Email | `remittance_email` | `remittance` |
| Optional Email | `optional_email` | `optional` |
| Accounting Email | `accounting_email` | `accounting` |

Customers edit these on Settings; staff edit the same matrix (plus Primary itself) on the admin customer's Billing & comms tab.

> **Staff note — new-account defaults:** on first enable, invoice emails, statements, and reminders are all ON (routed to Primary) and paper invoices/statements are OFF, with the enable timestamp as the cutoff so a newly enabled customer never gets a blast of their QBO history.

### Team management

Account admins invite coworkers, set roles (**Member / Admin / Accounts payable**), resend welcome emails, or remove access. Each person gets their own login — never share passwords. Staff manage the same from the admin customer detail → Users tab (invite / attach existing user / set role / detach).

## Applying for a new account (/apply)

The public application at https://orders.brixbev.com/apply is a full credit-app-grade form: business information (+ DUNS), tax ID and resale certificate with photo/PDF upload (**without a resale certificate, sales tax will be charged**), role contacts (Accounting / Billing / Orders / Management), delivery preferences with special instructions (gate codes, keys), multiple delivery locations, structured address fields with automatic ZIP verification ("Did you mean …"), an optional credit application (Net 15/Net 30, 3 trade references), and posted Terms & Conditions with a required drawn signature.

What happens after submit — approval, the SF customer create, the $0 setup ticket, and enabling the portal login — is the staff workflow in [SOP-2 · Customer Lifecycle](#/22-sop-customer-lifecycle) and [Brix Order /admin](#/03-brix-order-admin).
