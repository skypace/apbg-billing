Need a certificate of insurance?

Every COI request goes to our broker, Ram Risk Group, at coi@ramriskgroup.com — not to a carrier, and not to an individual. Send four things, or it comes back to you for more information:
Their full legal name and complete address. This is who the certificate is issued to, and it is the field most often sent incomplete.
What we are doing for them, if the contract calls for it — e.g. “beverage equipment installation and service at 123 Main St.”
Whatever their contract requires. Ours are listed below — check theirs against ours before you promise anything.
Additional Insured, Waiver of Subrogation, primary and non-contributory. Say so explicitly — see the warning below.
Ticking “additional insured” on the form is not the same as having the endorsement. The ACORD certificate says so itself: if the holder is an additional insured, or subrogation is waived, the policy must actually be endorsed — a line on the certificate confers nothing. If a contract requires it, ask Ram Risk for the endorsement, not just the certificate.
Their hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Anything sent after hours is handled the next business day, so a Friday-afternoon request for a Monday job start is a Tuesday certificate. Their office is in North Palm Beach, Florida — if those are Eastern hours, their 5 PM is our 2 PM, so treat early afternoon as the cut-off.

Spinnaker was cancelled on 29 August 2026. Great American is our general liability now. Four things follow from cancelling a policy mid-term, and none of them happens by itself:
  • Every certificate issued under Spinnaker is void going forward, and any additional-insured or waiver-of-subrogation endorsement written on it dies with the policy. Holders who require current evidence — the landlord (LPC / City of Alameda) first — need a reissued certificate from Ram Risk, and any contract requiring additional-insured status needs that endorsement re-done under Great American.
  • Check the cancellation date is on or after 25 August. The new policy incepted 8/25; backdating the cancellation earlier to recover more premium would open a window with no general liability at all.
  • Get the cancellation notice in writing and file it here. That document is what proves we were not carrying two policies, and it fixes the date if anyone asks later.
  • There should be a return premium. Confirm whether it is pro-rata or short-rate.
The archived Spinnaker record stays. It was an occurrence-form policy, so it still answers for anything that happened between 1 January and the cancellation date, even though it is no longer in force — a claim from that window is made against that policy, and the policy number is how you find it. Cancelled is not the same as never existed. Tick Show archived on the table above to see it.

Read this before you print the board

  • Which IWC Wage Order applies is still open. Order 1 (Manufacturing) indexes "Bottling (soft drinks)"; Order 7 (Mercantile) indexes "Wholesale houses"; Order 9 (Transportation) covers firms in the business of transport/warehousing for others — our own delivery drivers don't by themselves pull us in. DIR's test is the principal purpose of the business, and only one order applies unless operations are separately managed at every level. Confirm with DLSE before printing one.
  • Whistleblowers Are Protected must be on 8.5 × 14 legal paper with margins no wider than half an inch, so the type exceeds 14 point. A letter-size printout is non-compliant.
  • Form 300A posts February 1 – April 30 covering the prior year, even with zero injuries, and must be mailed or handed to route drivers and field techs who don't report weekly to the posting location.
  • "California Workplace — Know Your Rights" (SB 294) is a distribution duty. Every employee gets it by Feb 1 each year, in the language we normally use with them. Up to $500 per employee if we miss it.
  • Fill-in posters count only when filled in — Payday Notice, S-500 Emergency Phone Numbers, DWC-7 claims administrator, and the workers' comp carrier notice.
  • SB 553 workplace violence has no poster. The duty is a written plan, a violent-incident log, training, and records — the plan must be easily accessible to employees at all times.
  • Time Off to Vote is seasonal — up about ten days before each statewide election (next: post by ~Oct 24, 2026 for the Nov 3 general).

Any inspection — how to run the visit

  1. At the gate: ask for credentials. Log name, agency, badge number, arrival time.
  2. Page the escort and notify Sky (415) 713-3318. Nobody walks the site unescorted.
  3. Seat them at the records first — open Inspector Mode on the tablet and let them search. Volunteering the digital setup reframes it as a strength.
  4. Answer what is asked. "I'll confirm that and get back to you" is a complete answer. Don't narrate history or speculate.
  5. Never guess a quantity — pull the number up in the app.
  6. Photograph what they photograph. Write down each flagged item in their own words, in the moment.
  7. Correct trivially fixable things on the spot — an open container gets closed while they watch. Never move or conceal anything.
  8. Never sign an admission of fault. Signing receipt of a report is fine.

Before they leave — ask these four

  1. Which findings are violations and which are observations?
  2. Is anything classified serious / Class I, and on what basis?
  3. What is the correction deadline, and what proof of correction do you want?
  4. Will there be a reinspection, or is documentary proof sufficient?
