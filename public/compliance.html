How to run the inspection with this app 60-second user guide

  1. Open Inspector Mode on the tablet (first tab) and hand it over, or drive it yourself. Volunteering the digital setup up front reframes it as a strength.
  2. Inspector asks for a record → type a word in the search box (try CO2, insurance, permit). Documents and SDS filter together, instantly.
  3. Inspector asks for a chemical's SDS → search the product name → Open SDS → opens the manufacturer sheet. Offline fallbacks if internet drops: the SDS zip pack on this tablet, then the paper binder in the front office.
  4. Inspector asks about training → Safety Handbook & Sign-off tab → the signature roster IS the training record (name, date, version, signature). The handbook text above it is the training content.
  5. Need paper → 🖨 Print record index (Inspector Mode) prints clean.
  6. Editing during the walk: don't. Fix records after they leave — edits live in Refractor (link top-right), corrections to CERS at cers.calepa.ca.gov.

Say these two things FIRST — self-report before they find it

1 · The CO₂ inventory line understates the bulk tank. "Our CERS inventory line says 1,000 lb max in cylinders — it should read our 6-ton Airgas-managed bulk tank, 12,000 lb, aboveground tank plus cylinders, 365 days on site. Here's the tank's ASME U-1A data report (National Board #4319). The correction is being filed in CERS this week."

2 · The Business Activities page needs a used-oil update. "We've started dismantling coolers on site — compressor oil drained from that work makes us a used-oil generator, and our 8/10 filing answered 'No' on hazardous waste. We're correcting that, the oil is in labeled closed containers on containment, and here are the hauler receipts."

A correction the inspector hears from you first is diligence; the same fact discovered on the walk is a finding. Everything else in the filing is consistent — say these two and stop.

At the gate

  • Verify and log: name, agency, badge number, arrival time.
  • Notify Sky + Sloan by text immediately, whoever is on site escorts — nobody walks unescorted.
  • Seat them at the records first (this app, Inspector Mode) before the physical walk.

During the walk

  • Answer what is asked. "I'll confirm and get back to you" is a complete answer. Don't narrate history.
  • Never guess a quantity — pull it up in this app.
  • Photograph everything they photograph. Write down each flagged item in their words, in the moment.
  • Correct trivially fixable things on the spot (open container → close it while they watch). Never move or conceal anything.
  • Never sign an admission of fault. Signing receipt of a report is fine.

Before they leave — ask these four

  1. Which findings are violations vs observations?
  2. Is anything Class I, and on what basis?
  3. What's the correction deadline, and what proof do you want?
  4. Will there be a reinspection, or is documentary proof enough?

Afterwards: file their report + your notes in the vault same-day; every finding becomes a tracked corrective action within 48 hours; root-cause anything that traces to a gap in SOP-10 and amend it (full post-inspection table in SOP-10 §5).

Morning-of checklist

  • Tablet charged, signed in, this app open; SDS zip pack + inspection packet on it
  • CO₂ monitor + cellular backup showing normal (verified 8/17 — glance again)
  • Used-oil containers: labeled "Used Oil" + start date, closed, on containment; hauler receipts in one folder
  • Cylinders upright, chained, caps on; full/empty segregated; the indoor one-or-two within monitor coverage
  • Eyewash (main bathroom) clear and working; extinguishers tagged + unobstructed
  • Kem-Tek chlorine tablets stored dry, away from the acid ice-machine cleaners and bleach
  • Site map A1 + training signature sheet located and photographed
  • Aisles/exits clear; nothing stored against the CO₂ shutoff (#4461)
